## H1 (verbatim, hypothesis.md; dissertation Chapter 11 quote)

> "An MCF-conformant bridge (Protect = DID-gated, revocation-aware validator
> identity; Detect = sequential anomaly detection; Respond = on-chain
> rate-limited circuit breaker) bounds worst-case attacker extraction to
> T + r·Δ and detects low-and-slow exfiltration that a value-threshold baseline
> misses, at a false-positive cost of ≤ φ on benign bridge traffic."

> **J16 SUPERSESSION NOTE (Wave 4).** The pre-registered H1 above is quoted
> **verbatim, as registered**. Its Respond clause has since been **refuted**: the
> deterministic bound is not `T + r·Δ` but the arrival-curve envelope
> `X ≤ B + r·(t_trip + Δ) ≤ B + r·(W + Δ) = 4,660 ETH`, which does not involve `T`
> and is identical under the LOCKED and ALT_TRIP ledgers ([[theorem]] §14.1,
> `main.tex` Appendix A, `journal/THEOREM_1_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md`). Every `T + r·Δ`
> below is therefore a **limiter-only reference level**, never a bound, and the
> row-4 verdict is re-scored accordingly. The Detect and patient clauses are
> unaffected. H1 is reproduced unedited because rewriting a pre-registered
> hypothesis after the fact would destroy its purpose.

**Venue-neutral operative form (paper H1; same math, no MCF branding):**
A DID-anchored validator registry with cryptographic credential binding and
automatic expiry (Protect), combined with a windowed on-chain outflow bound and
sequential change-point detector (Detect + Respond), bounds worst-case attacker
extraction to `T + r·Δ` (fast, deterministic; **superseded by the envelope
`B + r·(W + Δ)`; see the J16 note above**) and `E[X_slow] ≤ B + r·D + r·Δ`
(patient, in expectation), and detects low-and-slow exfiltration that a static
value-threshold baseline misses, at a measured benign false-positive cost φ. The
architecture derives from the author's MSc MCF dissertation (unpublished); claims
stand independently of the framework. See `hypothesis.md` §Venue-neutral operative
form for the full provenance note.

## Governing Detect framing (hypothesis.md:51–70, quoted)

> "The sequential detector is a **low-false-positive streaming complement** that
> reduces response latency and catches structuring/low-and-slow drains that
> value thresholds miss; the loss guarantee is carried by the on-chain
> limiter/breaker, with the detector tightening the δ_detect term of Δ (and
> hence the `r·Δ` slack in `X ≤ T + r·Δ` and the `r·D` term in the expectation
> bound `E[X_slow] ≤ B + r·D + r·Δ`)."

*(Quoted as registered. `X ≤ T + r·Δ` is superseded; see the J16 note above. The
`r·Δ` slack the detector tightens is now the `r·Δ` inside the envelope
`B + r·(t_trip + Δ)`, and the argument is unchanged: a faster `δ_detect` shrinks
`Δ`, and an earlier `t_trip` shrinks the envelope directly.)*

Locked constants throughout: B=1000 ETH, r=1 ETH/s, T=5000 ETH, W=3600 s,
D=480 s, n=9, k=5, m=3 (`docs/logs/scenario_5_bound.log:3`,
`docs/bolt/theorem.md`).
