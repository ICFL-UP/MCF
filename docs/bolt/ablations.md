**δ/λ/cap selection (design decision, V2 2.3):** TRAIN is 100% benign, so
Youden-J is not computable on TRAIN alone. We adopt the **FPR-budget**
procedure: grid-search (λ, δ, cap) ∈ {4, 6, 8, 12, 16} × {0.25, 0.5, 1.0} ×
{1.5, 2.0, 3.0} (skipping cap ≤ δ); constraint φ_train ≤ 0.01 (benign flag
rate on the TRAIN stream); among admissible configs maximise power on
canonical drains *injected into the TRAIN cadence* (never TEST); tie-break on
lowest φ_train. Alternative (Youden-J on TRAIN+injections) rejected in one
line: it optimises a synthetic mixture ratio we would have to invent, whereas
the FPR budget is an operational quantity the reframed 2.1 claim already
commits to.

**Composite operating point (ALERT=3.0 / CRITICAL=6.0):** these two thresholds
are dissertation-fixed (§11.2.3) and deliberately kept out of the
train-selection scope — their FPR behaviour is already characterised
empirically by scenario_4 (`scenarios/scenario_4_fpr_sweep.py` probes the
alert/critical thresholds across four synthetic buckets). To meet the "every
parameter has a documented selection procedure" acceptance line honestly, the
ablation below adds an explicit ALERT-threshold axis (threshold ∈
{2.0, 3.0, 4.0, 6.0} at the default weights, φ + recall reported).
